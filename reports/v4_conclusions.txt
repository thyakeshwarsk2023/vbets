V-BETS v4 experiment conclusions (numbers from committed reports only)

1. Sample #10 diagnosis: across 10 initialization seeds, raw v2 predicted RED in 6/10 runs and v3 in 2/10. Both models flipped across seeds. In the five-seed leave-one-category-out analysis, Sample #10 was RED in 1/5 runs without C1, 0/5 without C2, and 0/5 without C3; full v3 was RED in 1/5 on those same five seeds. No individual category is implicated. The lower v3 recovery fraction versus v2 is associated with the v3 training run, but these results do not prove a causal mechanism.

2. V4 recovery and held-out RED recall: v4 predicted Sample #10 as RED in 3/5 initialization runs, compared with v3 in 1/5 on the Step 5 five-seed set. Mean held-out Config B RED recall was v4 94.1%, v3 95.6%, and v2 93.3%. Thus v4 did not preserve v3's mean held-out RED recall (1.5 percentage-point decrease), though it exceeded v2 by 0.8 percentage points. At initialization seed 42, Config B held-out RED recall was v4 25/27 (92.6%) and v3 27/27 (100%).

3. YELLOW recall: Config B split-seed mean was v2 89.1%, v3 89.1%, and v4 88.0%, a 1.1 percentage-point decrease for v4 versus both earlier versions. The range for v4 was 73.9%-100.0%. On the final synthetic set, both v4 and v2 recalled all 10/10 YELLOW cases.

4. Final fresh set: selected v4 and baseline v2 each scored RED recall 10/10 = 100.0%, exact 95% Clopper-Pearson CI [69.2%, 100.0%], and accuracy 30/30 = 100.0% in Configs A, B, and C. The point-estimate difference was 0 percentage points; the interval width was 30.8 percentage points, so the observed difference was not larger than the interval width. Both models made zero errors on this set.

5. Remaining limits: the fresh test has N=30 (10 per class), uses synthetic author-written sentences, and does not establish performance on real patients, other authors, populations, sites, accents, transcription conditions, or clinical workflows. The model remains an academic prototype and needs independent, adequately powered validation.

Report paragraph: In a pre-registered comparison, v4 was selected because it had the highest mean Config B RED recall across four split seeds (93.5%) and did not have any RED case missed in at least 3 of 5 initialization runs on the previously used contaminated set; v2 and v3 were excluded for repeatedly missing Sample #10. V4 predicted Sample #10 as RED in 3/5 initialization runs, but its mean held-out Config B RED recall (94.1%) was 1.5 percentage points below v3 (95.6%). On the single fresh synthetic test set (N=30), v4 and v2 both achieved 100% accuracy and 10/10 RED recall, with a 95% exact interval of 69.2%-100.0% for RED recall and no observed difference between them. These small, synthetic, author-written results do not establish clinical generalization.
